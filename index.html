Publications

  1. Beyond LLM Serving: Characterizing Vision-Language-Action Workloads for Embodied AI System Design
    Seonghun Jung, Sieun Moon, Jiyoung Jeong, Jimin Lee, and Jaehyuk Huh
    ASPLOS 2027
    Paper summary

    Problem. VLA inference runs inside a robot’s control loop, coupling latency and energy consumption with task success.

    Approach. Characterized four VLA architectures across an edge GPU server and two onboard SoCs using stage-level profiling, roofline analysis, DVFS sweeps, and 43,200 closed-loop episodes.

    Findings. Showed that model architecture and hardware balance shift stage bottlenecks and change the preferred operating point across latency, energy, and task-success objectives.

Projects

  1. ProfilingSense: A Non-Intrusive Kubernetes Scheduler for Distributed Deep Learning Workloads Based on Resource-Efficiency Profiling
    B.S. Graduation Project, advised by Prof. Euiseong Seo
    Sep. 2024 – May 2025
    Project summary

    Problem. Distributed training jobs must acquire all requested GPUs together, but Kubernetes accounts for neither gang scheduling nor differences in job-level resource efficiency.

    Approach. ProfilingSense profiles each job in an isolated pool, extracts iteration throughput from standard training logs, and ranks jobs by measured throughput per resource plus wait-time aging. It changes only job execution order, without modifying training code, sharing GPUs, or reallocating resources.

    Findings. Using profiles of five workloads measured on a 6-GPU testbed, we replayed 2,312 Microsoft Philly jobs at 864-GPU scale. Compared with FIFO-Gang scheduling, ProfilingSense reduced peak queue length by 8.2% and P99 JCT by 5.8%.
